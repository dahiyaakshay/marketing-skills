# Content: Landing Page Copywriter

Writes conversion-focused copy for a single-goal landing page — distinct from a blog post, this content exists to drive one specific action, and every element should serve that action.

**When invoking**: On first use, confirm the single conversion goal before drafting. On subsequent use, go straight to drafting.

## Scope

Copywriting for the page. Not structural/CRO audit of an existing page (see **landing-page-cro-audit**, paid category) or A/B test design (see **ab-test-planner**, paid category).

## Structural Elements

| Element | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Headline** | States the core value proposition in the reader's language, not internal jargon |
| **Subheadline** | Clarifies who it's for or what makes it credible |
| **Above-the-fold CTA** | The primary action, visible without scrolling |
| **Benefit-led body copy** | Organized around outcomes for the reader, not a feature list |
| **Social proof** | Testimonials, logos, or numbers that reduce perceived risk |
| **Objection handling** | Addresses the 2–3 most common hesitations directly, ideally as an FAQ or dedicated section |
| **Repeated CTA** | The same action reinforced at logical points further down the page, not just once at the top |

## One Goal Per Page

A landing page should drive exactly one conversion action — every competing link, secondary CTA, or unrelated navigation option dilutes the primary goal. If multiple audiences or offers are in play, that's a signal for multiple landing pages, not one page trying to serve all of them.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the single conversion goal and target audience.
2. Draft the headline and subheadline around the core value proposition.
3. Structure body copy around reader outcomes, not features.
4. Insert social proof and objection-handling sections.
5. Place the CTA above the fold and repeat it at natural points further down.
6. Route the finished page to **landing-page-cro-audit** for structural review.

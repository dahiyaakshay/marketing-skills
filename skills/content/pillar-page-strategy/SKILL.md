# Content: Pillar Page Strategy

Selects and structures the single central page for a topic the page every supporting/cluster page links back to, and that aims to be the definitive resource on the subject rather than ranking for every long-tail variant itself.

**When invoking**: On first use, briefly explain the selection criteria below. On subsequent use, go straight to the plan.

## Scope

Pillar selection and structure. Not the surrounding spoke architecture (see **topic-cluster-mapping**) or the keyword clustering that feeds this decision (see **keyword-clustering**).

## Selection Criteria

| Criterion | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Aggregate search volume** | The candidate cluster with the highest combined monthly volume across its keyword variants is usually the strongest pillar candidate |
| **Topical centrality** | Does this topic sit at the center of the site's niche, or at the edge? A pillar should anchor the site's core expertise, not a tangential subject |
| **Realistic competitiveness** | Can the site credibly compete for the head term at its current authority level? An overly ambitious pillar with no realistic path to ranking wastes the surrounding cluster's link equity |
| **Breadth of subtopics available** | A strong pillar topic should support at least several genuinely distinct cluster/spoke pages if there's only one or two related subtopics, it may not be broad enough to warrant a dedicated pillar |

## Pillar Page Structure

- Targets the head term directly; comprehensive enough to stand alone as a resource, not just a table of contents.
- Links out to every cluster/spoke page under it (see **topic-cluster-mapping** for the full linking pattern).
- Refreshed on a regular cadence a pillar's freshness signal matters more than a typical page's, since it anchors an entire cluster's authority; a decaying pillar page pulls down the ranking potential of everything linking to it.

## Workflow

1. Review candidate clusters from **keyword-clustering** and score each against the four selection criteria.
2. Choose the pillar topic with the strongest combination of volume, centrality, and realistic competitiveness.
3. Outline the pillar page to comprehensively cover the topic at a summary level, with clear hand-offs to each spoke for depth.
4. Plan the refresh cadence for the pillar specifically more frequent than a typical spoke page, given its structural importance.
5. Hand off the full spoke architecture to **topic-cluster-mapping**.

- **keyword-clustering**: Upstream keyword grouping that surfaces pillar candidates

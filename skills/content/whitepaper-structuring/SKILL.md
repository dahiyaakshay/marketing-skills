# Content: Whitepaper Structuring

Structures a long-form research whitepaper for credibility and readability the standard that separates a whitepaper readers trust from one that reads as thin marketing dressed up in a longer format.

**When invoking**: On first use, briefly note the honesty-over-persuasion principle below. On subsequent use, go straight to structuring.

## Scope

Structural and methodological framing for whitepapers/research reports. Not on-page SEO scoring (see **content-optimizer**) or AI-citation-specific structuring (see **ai-citation-optimization**, geo category) apply both alongside this skill for a whitepaper meant to perform in both traditional and AI search.

## Standard Structure

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Executive summary / key findings** | States the core finding(s) up front, in plain language, before the methodology |
| **Methodology** | Honest, specific description of how the research was actually conducted sample size, data source, timeframe, and any real limitations, stated plainly rather than hidden or inflated |
| **Findings** | Organized by theme or research question, not chronologically by "what we did first" |
| **Discussion/implications** | What the findings mean for the reader's decisions, distinct from the raw findings themselves |
| **Limitations** | An honest section naming what the research doesn't establish omitting this is the fastest way to lose credibility with a sophisticated reader |

## Credibility Principles

- **Describe methodology honestly and specifically** rather than using statistical language that implies more rigor than was actually applied a simple correlation described as a simple correlation is more trustworthy than the same finding dressed up with inapplicable statistical terminology.
- **Avoid first-study or novelty claims** unless they can be substantiated; overclaiming novelty is one of the fastest ways to undermine an otherwise solid piece of research once a knowledgeable reader checks the claim.
- **State limitations directly** a whitepaper that only presents strengths reads as promotional rather than as research.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the actual methodology used and describe it plainly and specifically no statistical language beyond what was actually done.
2. Draft the executive summary stating key findings up front.
3. Organize findings thematically, not as a narrative of the research process.
4. Write an honest limitations section.
5. Cross-check for any novelty or "first" claims and verify they're substantiated before keeping them.
6. Apply **content-optimizer** for on-page scoring and **ai-citation-optimization** for AI-answer-engine structure as separate passes.

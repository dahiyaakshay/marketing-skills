# Local: Local Landing Page Builder

Writes a location-specific landing page that's genuinely differentiated for its city/region — not a templated page with only the city name swapped, which both underperforms for ranking and reads poorly to local visitors.

**When invoking**: On first use, briefly note the differentiation requirement below. On subsequent use, go straight to drafting.

## Scope

Individual location page copy and structure. For the architecture governing many such pages across a multi-location brand, see **multi-location-seo**. For general landing page principles, see **landing-page-copywriter** (content category).

## Why Templated "Swap the City Name" Pages Underperform

A location page that's identical to every other location page except for the city name is easily identified as thin, duplicate-pattern content — both by search engines evaluating quality across a domain and by local visitors who can tell the content wasn't actually written for their area. Genuine local relevance (neighborhood references, region-specific services or considerations, local testimonials, actual local team/location details) is what differentiates a location page that ranks and converts from one that doesn't.

## Structural Elements

| Element | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Location-specific intro** | References the actual service area, neighborhood, or region-specific context, not a generic opener |
| **Local team/location details** | Photos, staff names, or specific location details unique to this branch, where applicable |
| **Local testimonials/reviews** | Feedback from customers in that specific location, not generic company-wide testimonials reused everywhere |
| **Region-specific service notes** | Any genuinely different service offerings, pricing, or considerations specific to that location |
| **Local schema markup** | LocalBusiness schema for that specific location (see **local-schema-markup**) |
| **NAP for that location** | Consistent with the canonical format from **local-citation-audit** |

## Workflow

1. Gather genuinely location-specific details (team, service area nuances, local testimonials) rather than defaulting to templated copy.
2. Draft the page around those specifics, avoiding a "find and replace city name" pattern.
3. Add LocalBusiness schema specific to that location.
4. Confirm NAP consistency with the canonical format.
5. Cross-link to and from other location pages appropriately (see **multi-location-seo** for the overall architecture).

# Local: Review Response Writer

Drafts owner responses to customer reviews, calibrated by review sentiment Google treats timely, relevant, personalized responses as a trust and engagement signal, so response quality has both a reputational and a ranking dimension.

**When invoking**: On first use, confirm the review's sentiment and any specific complaint/praise to address. On subsequent use, go straight to drafting.

## Scope

Individual review response drafting. Not the broader GBP optimization this feeds into (see **gbp-optimizer**).

## Response Principles by Sentiment

| Sentiment | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Positive** | Thank genuinely and specifically reference what the reviewer mentioned rather than a generic "thanks for your review"; avoid a copy-paste template that reads the same across every response |
| **Negative, legitimate complaint** | Acknowledge the specific issue without being defensive, state what will be done (or was done) to address it, and invite further contact offline for resolution the response is also being read by future prospective customers, not just the reviewer |
| **Negative, unfair or inaccurate** | Respond calmly and factually, correct the specific inaccuracy without being combative, and avoid arguing publicly an overly defensive or combative response often damages the business's image more than the original review did |
| **Fake or policy-violating review** | Flag for removal through the platform's reporting process rather than responding publicly as though it were legitimate |

## Why Personalization Matters

A generic, templated response even a polite one reads as inauthentic to anyone reading the review thread later, and Google's local-ranking treatment of review responses favors ones that appear timely, relevant, and specifically personalized rather than boilerplate. Reference specifics from the actual review whenever possible.

## Workflow

1. Read the review carefully and identify its sentiment and specific content.
2. Draft a response that references specifics from the review rather than a generic template.
3. For negative reviews, acknowledge the issue directly and offer a path to resolution, keeping the tone measured for the benefit of future readers.
4. For clearly fake or policy-violating reviews, flag for platform removal rather than responding as if legitimate.
5. Respond promptly response timeliness itself is part of the engagement signal.

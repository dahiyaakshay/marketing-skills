# Project Context

Fill this in and place it at `.claude/project-context.md` or `.cursor/project-context.md`. Skills read it automatically. Start with sections 1–4 and 8; add 5–7 as they become available. Update regularly stale context degrades output quality.

## 1. Product
What the product/service is, in 2–3 sentences.

## 2. Audience
Who buys it, their role/title, and their level of technical sophistication.

## 3. Brand Voice
Tone (formal/casual), regional spelling (British/American English), banned words or punctuation (e.g. no em dashes), formatting conventions.

## 4. Primary Keywords / Topics
The core terms and topics this project should rank and be cited for.

## 5. Competitors
Direct competitors to benchmark content and rankings against.

## 6. Existing Content Inventory
Link or path to a sitemap / content list, if one exists.

## 7. Known Issues
Any known technical SEO issues, past penalties, or migration history worth knowing before auditing.

## 8. Goals
What success looks like traffic, citations, leads, rankings for specific terms and the timeframe.
